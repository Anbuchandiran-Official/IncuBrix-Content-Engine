**Results:** not run. No participant responses or owned-account publishing results were available. This file contains a ready-to-run preference test; it is not evidence of audience validation.

## Run a preference test

Administer the three concepts below to at least 20 consenting, eligible adults in your existing panel in randomized order. Collect no names or sensitive personal information. Record consent, eligibility, date, preference, action intent, and a short anonymized reason.

### Test stimuli

1. **Repurposing craft:** “One source. Five useful angles. No copy-paste carousel.” A five-panel guide turns one recording into a how-to, audience FAQ, lesson, and platform-specific post.
2. **Creator operations:** “A good repurposing brief starts with one sentence.” A practical prompt to define the source’s core promise before editing.
3. **Audience value:** “A clip needs context before it needs captions.” A short-video concept that adds the original question and a clear takeaway before styling captions.

These are educational concepts, not IncuBrix product demonstrations.

### Ask each participant

- Which concept is most useful to you? (1, 2, or 3)
- Which would you be most likely to save, share, or click? (choose any)
- What made you choose it? (optional, remove personal details)
- Are you a creator, editor, or part of a small content team? (optional)
- Do you consent to your anonymous answers being counted in this assessment? (required)

### Report results honestly

Use the existing panel only; do not contact creators or brands for this assessment. Record channel, dates, eligibility, sample size, response counts, and consent. Keep answers anonymous: do not collect names, contact details, or identifying information. Report each concept's count and proportion using the eligible, consenting response denominator. Describe selection bias and missing answers. Do not claim statistical representativeness or compare against a benchmark unless the benchmark and method are cited.
