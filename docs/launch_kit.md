# 30-day launch kit

**Proposed start:** Monday, 26 October 2026. **Timezone:** Singapore Time (UTC+8). **Channels:** LinkedIn, Instagram, YouTube Shorts. This is a launch-ready plan; it has not been scheduled or published because account access and owner approval were not supplied.

## Before day one

1. IncuBrix owner confirms the public signup destination, product claims, visual identity, and account permissions.
2. Product reviewer signs off any product demonstration against the live app. The current posts are educational and avoid unverified product claims.
3. Growth lead imports `content/calendar.csv` into the native schedulers and confirms each planned slot and media file.
4. Analyst tests the UTM link and records a baseline from the last 30 days of owned-account analytics.
5. Run the preference test described in `audience_test.md` before claiming audience validation.

## Publishing plan

Publish one asset each day for 30 days, rotating 10 posts per channel. The CSV contains the planned date and 10:00 a.m. Singapore slot. These are suggested slots, not posts already scheduled on a platform. Adjust slot timing using the account’s own data after week one.

| Week | Focus | Daily actions |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Establish baseline and test hooks | Publish 7; check every link; reply to comments; log 24-hour reach and interactions. |
| 2 | Test format and creator problem | Publish 7; compare video, carousel, and text-post saves/clicks. |
| 3 | Add real creator perspective | Publish 7; only use participant/creator material with written consent. |
| 4 | Repeat useful formats and close the loop | Publish 7; review sign-ups and best/worst quartiles. |
| Days 29–30 | Decide next cycle | Publish remaining 2; record results and owners for the next calendar. |

## Working targets

These are proposed first-cycle operating targets, not historical IncuBrix results or industry benchmarks. Replace them after the first week’s baseline if account data supports a different target.

| Metric | First-cycle target | Definition |
|---|---:|---|
| Reach | 15,000 total impressions | Sum of native impressions across 30 posts; avoid double-counting unique people across platforms. |
| Engagement rate | 3% average | (Reactions + comments + shares + saves, where available) / impressions. Report per platform. |
| Outbound clicks | 150 | UTM-attributed landing-page sessions; use platform link clicks as a cross-check. |
| Sign-ups | 10 | Completed registrations attributed to campaign UTMs; exclude starts and duplicate accounts. |
| Tracking coverage | 100% | Every published asset has a tested UTM and a 24-hour plus 7-day record. |

Targets are directional planning values only. No audience or performance results have been collected yet.

## Daily workflow

1. Confirm the source and audience problem; generate/edit copy in the content engine.
2. Verify factual and product claims; product content needs a dated evidence record.
3. Check caption, dimensions, subtitles, alt text, link, UTM, and usage rights.
4. Schedule in the platform’s native scheduler and record the final post URL.
5. At 24 hours and 7 days, record reach/impressions, interactions, outbound clicks, and attributed sign-ups.
6. Each Monday, compare formats within each platform and update the next week’s content.

## Risks and controls

- **Product claim drift:** product reviewer must approve every demo; remove claims that cannot be shown.
- **No account access:** do not publish until an owner confirms permissions; use the demo app only for software review.
- **Rights:** current graphics and animations use original text/vector shapes and system fonts, with no third-party audio, faces, or stock media. Obtain consent before creator collaborations.
- **Low sample size:** report denominators and keep tests open longer; do not claim a winner from a handful of views.
- **Tracking failure:** open each UTM URL and validate the analytics event before scheduling.
- **No integrations:** this project prepares content and exports a calendar; it does not publish to social accounts automatically.
