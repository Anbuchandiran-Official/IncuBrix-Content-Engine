# Social growth strategy

## Objective

Help creator-led businesses and their small teams learn repeatable ways to turn one useful idea into consistent, audience-first content. Use social posts to earn attention, invite a low-friction next step, and measure qualified visits and sign-ups. This strategy does not assume any particular IncuBrix feature; product demonstrations must be added only after direct verification.

## Audience

Primary: independent podcasters, YouTubers, and expert-led creators in the US, Canada, UK, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, and Singapore. Secondary: editors and small content teams who help creators plan, repurpose, and publish. Common problems to validate in interviews: inconsistent publishing, too much manual repurposing, unclear performance signals, and maintaining a recognizable voice across channels.

## Initial channel mix

- **LinkedIn:** useful for expert-led creators and editors who share practical workflows and professional lessons. Lead with specific, saveable advice and discussion prompts.
- **Instagram:** supports visual explainers, carousels, and short vertical video. Use concise demonstrations and process breakdowns.
- **YouTube Shorts:** gives short educational video a searchable, video-first home and lets a creator test hooks and explanations.

The 30-day plan has 10 posts per channel, rotated across the month. Use the prepared post times as test slots, then change them using account analytics. Do not cross-post without adapting format and opening hook.

## Content pillars

1. **Repurposing craft:** show how to extract distinct ideas from one source.
2. **Creator operations:** simple planning, review, and publishing systems.
3. **Audience value:** teach hooks, story structure, accessibility, and usefulness.
4. **Measurement:** explain UTMs, meaningful engagement, and learning loops without implying guaranteed growth.
5. **Product proof:** only verified, screen-recorded workflows after product access; currently a reserved pillar, not a claim.

## Voice and production rules

- Direct, practical, and respectful of creators' time.
- Show one useful idea per post; avoid generic hustle language and guaranteed outcomes.
- Use plain language, readable captions, descriptive alt text, and on-screen subtitles for video.
- Product claims require a dated observation and screenshot or recording in `docs/research_log.md`.
- Use original text and diagrams, or assets with documented licenses. Obtain consent for faces, voices, and testimonials. Disclose synthetic media when required by a platform.
- Video drafts are 15–45 seconds, vertical 9:16, with a verbal/on-screen hook in the first two seconds. Carousels use 4–6 panels, 4:5 where supported.

## Conversion path

Useful post → one relevant CTA → tracked landing page or sign-up page → measure click and completed sign-up. Set UTMs per asset (`utm_source`, `utm_medium=social`, `utm_campaign=2027_growth`, `utm_content=<asset_id>`). The calendar links to the public IncuBrix home page; the owner must confirm the preferred signup destination and analytics event before launch. Report reach, engagement rate, outbound clicks, and completed sign-ups. Define engagement rate consistently as (reactions + comments + shares + saves, where available) / impressions.

## First five observations and three opportunities

Five observations of the current public site and three evidence-informed opportunities are documented in `docs/product_research.md`. They remain hypotheses until IncuBrix grants product access and audience tests produce results. Do not treat them as findings:

1. Show one verified workflow from source to platform-ready output; compare qualified clicks with educational tips.
2. Pair a practical workflow post with one tracked signup destination; compare qualified sessions and completed registrations.
3. Co-create a recurring workflow lesson with a consenting creator; compare saves, qualified clicks, and concept preference with solo tutorials.
