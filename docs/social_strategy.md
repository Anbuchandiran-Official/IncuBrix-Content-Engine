# Social strategy

The working strategy is in [strategy.md](strategy.md). It proposes creators and small content teams as the audience and LinkedIn, Instagram, and YouTube Shorts as initial channels. The five pillars are repurposing craft, creator operations, audience value, measurement, and verified product proof.

**Evidence status:** audience and platform hypotheses need validation. Product-proof content stays disabled until direct product observations are recorded in `product_research.md`.

## Funnel

Useful educational post → one relevant CTA → verified landing page with UTM tags → record first-party clicks and sign-ups. Baseline targets only after actual account analytics exist.
