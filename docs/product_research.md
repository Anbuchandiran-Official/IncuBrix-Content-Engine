## Three growth opportunities to test

1. **Show one verified workflow, end to end.** Publish a short screen-recorded walkthrough that labels each step and shows the actual output. The official site describes Insights, Creation, and Publishing as live, while Performance is marked “Rolling out now.” Measure qualified landing-page clicks and sign-ups per impression. This is a proposed test, not a result.
2. **Resolve the proof gap between broad positioning and a concrete next step.** Pair a practical creator workflow post with one clear CTA to the current free-start page. Track each asset with UTMs and measure landing-page visits and completed sign-ups. The CTA and conversion path need an IncuBrix owner to verify before launch.
3. **Build a recurring creator-workflow series with creator input.** Invite a podcaster, YouTuber, or editor to share a real workflow, then adapt the lesson into a post, carousel, and short video with consent. Test saves, qualified clicks, and preference against a solo tutorial. No collaboration or audience test has been run.

These opportunities are hypotheses inferred from the public messaging and account sample. They are not performance findings.
